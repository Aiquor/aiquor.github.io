## Landing-page assets

Store landing-page images and other display assets in [/assets](assets/). Store generated illustrations in [/assets/illustrations](assets/illustrations/) with descriptive filenames, and record their generation prompts there. Use the image model to create illustrations that match [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and the content they represent. Keep project-referenced assets inside this repository.

Preserve the hero imagery, page structure, layout, and animations when replacing non-hero stock images.
