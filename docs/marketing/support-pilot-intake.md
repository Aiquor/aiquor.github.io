# Aiquor Support Pilot — discovery and acceptance worksheet

Use this worksheet to qualify a client for the US$750 pilot before accepting payment. The offer is in `support-pilot-offer.md`.

## Discovery questions

1. Which Gmail mailbox receives support inquiries? Is it a regular mailbox, a delegated mailbox, or a Google Group?
2. Approximately how many new inquiries arrive each day, and which three questions recur most often?
3. Can the pilot focus on English product FAQs, shipping policy, and returns policy?
4. What proportion of inquiries requires live orders, stock, refunds, or customer-specific information?
5. Who owns and approves the FAQ? Is it within 20 pages / 10,000 words?
6. Who will review drafts, provide feedback during the build, and sign off acceptance?
7. Can you provide a development sample and 50 separate anonymized evaluation inquiries?
8. What tone and wording should replies use? Are there claims or promises the agent must never make?
9. Which AI provider and client-owned hosting environment can we use? Who can authorize access and billing?
10. What monthly spending cap and evaluation-data retention period are acceptable?
11. Is a five-business-day build practical once the prerequisites are supplied?
12. How will we time at least 10 matched inquiries to compare manual work with draft review?

## Fit decision

Proceed at US$750 only when all of the following are true:

- One compatible Gmail mailbox, one English FAQ source, and one deployment environment.
- The three included inquiry categories represent a useful first workflow.
- A human will review and send every reply.
- The client can provide the source content, anonymized examples, access, and a named reviewer.
- The workflow is useful without real-time store/order data.
- The third-party costs, data handling, spending cap, and acceptance targets are agreed.

If not, propose a smaller scope or a separately priced discovery/integration project. Do not promise order-status answers using only a policy FAQ.

## Kickoff record

Record the client/business name, authorized approver, mailbox, category definitions, FAQ version and approval date, AI provider, deployment environment, spending cap, data-retention decision, agreed start date, and target handover date. Keep credentials out of this document.

## Evaluation record

Create one row per held-out inquiry with:

- Anonymized case ID
- Expected category or mandatory escalation
- Expected facts / approved source passage
- Observed outcome
- Draft produced: yes/no
- Draft accepted without substantive factual changes: yes/no
- Unsupported facts or promises: yes/no, with notes
- Reviewer name and review date
- Initial result and retest result, if applicable

Keep 35 in-scope cases and 15 mandatory-escalation cases. Count missing drafts as unsuccessful drafts. Preserve failed cases and record what was corrected.
