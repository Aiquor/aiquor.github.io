# Aiquor: AI drafts. Your team decides.

15-second promotional walkthrough at `/demo/support/`, linked from the homepage
support case study and its clickable demo preview. All data and approvals are
fictional. Playback is scripted; no message is sent.

## Voiceover and storyboard

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Jamie asks about order MS-1042. | A customer asks: where is my order? |
| 3–7s | Order context and a short AI draft appear. | AI checks the order and drafts a reply. |
| 7–11s | Maya reviews, then approves at 9 seconds. | Your team reviews and approves. |
| 11–15s | The approved reply appears in the simulated inbox. | The customer gets an answer. Aiquor. AI drafts. You decide. |

## Recording

Open the demo at a desktop width, press Play demo, and keep the tab visible
while recording. Use the short voiceover above, or share silently with the
built-in captions. Keep the fictional-data label visible. The 15-second
presentation timing is not a processing-speed claim.

Suggested post: “From support request to approved reply in a 15-second demo.
AI drafts. Your team decides. Fictional data, real workflow design.”

## Playback

Play/pause, restart, seek, and chapter navigation work with a keyboard. Approval
appears at 9 seconds and the reply at 11 seconds. Playback stops at 15 seconds.
Reduced-motion preferences disable entrance transitions. No live AI, Slack,
order system, or email service is connected.
