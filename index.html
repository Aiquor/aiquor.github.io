What we build

We do five things. Most projects combine two or three.

  • AI agents & automation

    Agents that take over the repetitive parts of your operations: triage, drafting, data entry, follow-ups. We scope each one to a single job, test it against your real data, and keep a person in the loop for decisions that need one.

    Good fit for

    • Support and inbox triage
    • Report and document generation
    • Data cleanup and enrichment

  • MCP integrations

    Model Context Protocol servers that connect AI assistants like Claude to your own systems: your database, CRM, docs, or internal APIs. Each server checks permissions and logs every call, so you can hand it to the whole team.

    Good fit for

    • Letting your team ask questions of internal data
    • Exposing a legacy system to AI tools
    • Custom tools for Claude, Cursor, or your own agents

  • Custom Slack apps

    Bots and workflows that live where your team already works. Approvals, alerts, standups, on-call handoffs, or a front end for one of the agents above.

    Good fit for

    • Approval and request workflows
    • Alerts from your systems, with context
    • Asking an AI agent questions in a channel

  • Web development

    Fast, accessible websites and internal web apps, hand-built on modern tooling. We handle hosting, forms, analytics, and the parts people forget.

    Good fit for

    • Marketing sites that need to load fast
    • Client portals and internal dashboards
    • Replacing a site nobody can update anymore

  • Cybersecurity

    Practical security for teams without a security department. We find what is exposed, fix the highest-risk items first, and set up the basics so it stays fixed, including the AI tools you are adopting.

    Good fit for

    • Security reviews of apps, cloud accounts, and AI integrations
    • Access control, secrets, and MFA rollouts
    • Hardening before a compliance or customer audit
